How the Bot Learns a Website

After you enter the address, the bot crawls the site. Each page is added to the knowledge base, and the dashboard shows what exactly it has read. Facts not found on the site can be added as separate blocks: conditions, prices, ready answers to frequently asked questions.

From product cards, the bot compiles a catalog with prices and characteristics. If there are more products than pages crawled, the catalog is uploaded from Excel or synchronized via API. For example, for clinics and car services, the catalog consists of services; for me, it's pricing tiers: eight items, from a free tier to a partnership program.

The bot also fills out the business profile based on the website: operating hours, contacts, payment, delivery, returns, and warranties.

Using the same pages, the bot creates a sales script: conversation steps from greeting to lead capture, and the conditions under which it moves from one step to another. The script considers the industry and how the company sells: for a clinic, the conversation leads to booking an appointment; for a store, to an order; for a manufacturer, to contact for a quote. For my service, it resulted in five steps: greeting, clarifying the task, selecting a plan, contact for launch assistance, and completion. Any step can be rewritten or a new one added. The script dictates the conversation's direction, and the bot will answer a direct question at any step.

You don't need to write prompts. We built a salesperson: I worked with a partner who himself sells and talks to clients. Therefore, what usually needs to be written manually is already built-in: the bot speaks like a knowledgeable manager, keeps responses to two-three sentences, guides to the next step after answering, and doesn't conduct an interrogation. In most builders, the owner configures the role, tone, and funnel themselves, but here the bot is ready to respond immediately after crawling the site. You only need to edit the script and texts if you want to customize it.

The same bot with the same knowledge base responds in Telegram, MAX, WhatsApp, Instagram, and Avito, and sends leads to email, Telegram, Bitrix24, or Hollihop. If a human is needed, the manager joins the conversation from the dashboard, the bot pauses during this time, and the 'Return AI' button hands the dialogue back.

The full setup takes just a few minutes.

What Can Be Configured

Almost everything the bot has gathered and decided on its own can be adjusted manually. The script is edited on a diagram: steps and arrows between them, each step having its own type (clarification, explanation, objection, offer, contact, or custom), and the transition condition is written in words, for example, 'visitor wants to discuss the next step.' The response style is set by rules in plain text: answer concisely, do not use emojis, state the price first, write in English. These apply across all channels. Product cards and any profile fields can be edited manually.

The widget is customized to match the site: an input bar at the bottom center, a round icon or button in the corner with its own label, a predefined color or a custom HEX, separate spacing at the bottom for desktop and mobile.

The conversation start is also configurable. The greeting can be rewritten, and separate greetings can be created for ad campaigns: up to ten rules based on UTM tags, and a visitor from a specific ad will see their personalized first text. Quick questions appear as buttons in the chat, there are three of them, and each can have a predefined answer: it's sent immediately and doesn't consume message limits, and if the field is left empty, the model will respond. After a set number of seconds, the widget automatically displays a greeting or expands the chat, no more than once a day. For a visitor who doesn't respond or leave contact info, it reminds them with your text.

A visitor can attach a file in txt, md, or docx format to their message, for example, a list of what they need, and the bot will read it. Widget events are sent as goals to Yandex.Metrica: chat opening, first message, clicking a quick question, and leaving contact information. Leads and daily statistics are available in the dashboard.

How I Taught It Not to Lie

The first version worked like most neural network chatbots: a search found several similar fragments on the site, and the model generated an answer based on them. This was sufficient for questions about delivery. For product-related questions, the model confused prices of adjacent items and recommended things not in the catalog.

Now, the model doesn't search for products. For each visitor's reply, it writes a short program in our internal query language, and the code verifies and executes the program against the catalog and knowledge base. Conditions are recorded as a tree of logical operations, much like in SQL.

The model then generates an answer based on the program's results: using the found items and calculated facts, for example, how many options fit and which one is cheaper. The search under the program understands Russian morphology, so 'белая' (white, feminine) and 'белые' (white, plural) both find the value 'Белый' (White). It also understands paraphrasing: a visitor writes 'лючок' (hatch/small door), and the item in the catalog is called 'канализационный шлюз' (sewer hatch), and the bot finds it.

For each release, regression testing is performed on client cases. There are over 200 tests for various scenarios: it currently solves 197 or 198 of them, achieving an accuracy of about 99%.

How to Install It

  • On the SalesAgent website, click 'Create AI Salesperson' and paste your website address. Registration requires your name, phone number, email, and password.

  • Wait for the bot to read your site. It read my site in a minute; you can see the status on the 'Site' tab.

  • Conduct a test: ask about your prices as a client would. If the answer is incorrect, look for the relevant information in the 'Knowledge Base' tab. It's usually not there because the page wasn't crawled or is blocked from search engines, so you add the fact manually on the 'Manual' tab.

  • On the 'Widget' tab, copy the code snippet and paste it into your website before the </body> tag. For Tilda and WordPress, there's a dedicated field in the settings.

  • On the 'Integrations' tab, select where to send leads. Don't skip this step. Over 40% of phone numbers collected by the bot during the summer and early autumn remained in the dashboards because owners didn't get to this tab.

  • If you need unlimited bot usage, go to 'Your Model.' The key is issued in the DeepSeek dashboard under API keys or in the GLM console. Select a provider, paste the key, and click 'Connect Key': SalesAgent will send a single test request to the provider and activate the key if it responds.
