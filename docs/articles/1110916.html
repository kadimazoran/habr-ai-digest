I recently saw a study from Ahrefs: brands with ten times more mentions on web pages have about 3.7 times more mentions in AI responses — significantly less than one might expect from such a difference. When choosing platforms for brand mentions, it's important to consider that Ahrefs only studied third-party pages with search traffic. The persuasiveness of the link to AI responses is also important: the authors compared different companies, so the coefficient itself does not justify purchasing publications.

What Ahrefs Considered a Brand Mention in Neural Networks

A brand mention in an AI response means its name appeared in the response at least once. According to the Mentions metric rules in Brand Radar, a response with three occurrences of the name is counted as one mention.

In a study from November 3, 2026, Ahrefs compared data on 10,000 brands with responses to 260 million queries. They studied ChatGPT and two Google systems: AI Overviews and AI Mode. Queries with the name of the brand under study were excluded: they counted its appearance where the user did not mention the company.

It is important to distinguish between a brand mention and a source link. For the Citation metric, Ahrefs counts a domain if at least one of its pages is present in the response sources. The company name in the response and a link to its website are considered different indicators. The 3.7 ratio refers specifically to brand mentions in responses.

The presence of a name in a response does not yet mean that the AI recommends choosing that company.

What Third-Party Pages with Brand Mentions Did Ahrefs Consider

Ahrefs searched for brand names on external web pages using Content Explorer, considering only pages that, according to Ahrefs's estimate, receive visitors from regular search. The company's own website was excluded. Therefore, a web mention in this study cannot be equated with any appearance of the name on the internet.

In the description of the Ahrefs method, it is specified that the name was searched for by exact phrase match. To reduce the number of false positives, the authors kept names five characters or longer and excluded common words and personal names.

For placement selection, the traffic filter is particularly important. Suppose a contractor offers to publish material on a new page. Until it exists, it's impossible to check if it receives visitors from regular search. The number of promised publications itself does not allow considering future pages as analogues of the pages from the Ahrefs sample.

What Mentions Are More Closely Linked to Brand Appearance in AI Responses

The closest link in the Ahrefs study is to mentions in Reddit threads that appear in Google search results: a coefficient of about 0.75. A thread is a discussion with participant responses.

This is Spearman's rank correlation: it shows how consistently the order of brands changes across two metrics. If brands with a large number of web mentions usually have more mentions in AI, the correlation is positive. A value of 0.75 does not mean a 75% probability of appearing in a response, nor a 75% increase in recommendations.

For the number of mentioning pages and their total traffic, Ahrefs obtained the same combined correlation: 0.70. It's tempting to declare that page traffic is no longer important. However, pages that, according to Ahrefs's estimate, do not receive visitors from regular search were not included in the web count at all: the comparison already started with a filtered set.

The difference between 0.70 and 0.67 also does not imply that a regular publication will pay off better than a media article. The table helps choose a hypothesis about the platform, but not calculate its ROI.

How to Use Ahrefs Data When Choosing Company Publications

As initial directions for testing, the Ahrefs authors suggest Reddit and presence on various regular websites. They explicitly state that they cannot establish a causal link between mentions and brand appearance in AI responses.

The 10 to 3.7 ratio describes the difference between brands in the sample. Companies with ten times more web mentions had approximately 3.7 times more mentions in AI. The number 3.7 cannot be used as a forecast for the results of new placements for a single company.

The authors also compared brands within groups with similar search query frequency to roughly account for company size. This is a useful clarification, but equal search demand does not turn a comparison of companies into an experiment with publications.

For a company deciding how to get into neural networks, evaluating a contractor's offer depends on whether the page exists. If the name is added to an already published material, it is worth asking the contractor for Ahrefs's estimate of the number of visitors from regular search specifically for that page. For a new publication, compliance with the Ahrefs sample cannot yet be verified: visits from search can only be assessed after publication. A promise to release more materials does not answer the question of whether these pages will resemble those considered by Ahrefs.

The choice of AI service is also important. Alice AI and GigaChat were not included in the study. Even among the systems studied, the link varied: for six out of seven factors, ChatGPT was less strongly linked to external mentions than the two Google systems. The overall coefficient cannot be automatically transferred to a separate service.

A marketer makes the decision to purchase publications based on the results of placements for their company. The value of the Ahrefs study is that it helps choose types of mentions to test; the link between their quantity and brand appearance in AI responses does not prove the ROI of purchasing.
