Hello, Habr!

AI is entering production faster than teams can build defenses around it, and Russia already has Federal Law 243-FZ on AI, with growing demands for secure development. We discussed all of this on September 22nd at PRO DevSecOps Conf 2026, the annual conference by Swordfish Security Group on secure development and protected AI.

More than 30 speakers shared their experience, including representatives from the Bank of Russia, ISP RAS, Gazprombank, Russian Post, and VimpelCom.

At the conference, we:

  • discussed how mature teams conduct secure development and implement AI in practice;

  • explained what new requirements have emerged for those developing and implementing AI systems;

  • presented the updated AI Security SAIMM v2.0 maturity model;

  • analyzed real-world cases of secure pipelines at the scale of large companies;

  • heard firsthand from the product managers of AppSec Solutions about which tasks each tool addresses in practice;

  • and even learned what the calculation of a route in the open ocean has in common with risk management in work projects.
