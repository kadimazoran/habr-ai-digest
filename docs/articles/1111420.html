Where the funnel broke

At first, I thought the problem was with the emails the agent wrote when responding. I asked it to try different approaches, but to write only the truth. As a result, it rewrote them three times, removed polite phrases, added specifics about the team and workload, and shortened them to one screen. But none of this is needed by anyone: in a month, the resume was opened in three out of fourteen cases. The other eleven responses didn't even reach this stage.

The agent, by the way, searched very well, but between "sent" and "talked to a person" there is a filter that doesn't let me pass, and it most likely doesn't filter by email at all. It either filters by resume, or cuts everyone off. As an option, one could try creating a resume for each vacancy and specifying keywords from the vacancy there – but that's utter nonsense if it really works that way.

Second: responses come quickly and without review. One company rejected me after six days, another after twelve hours, opening the resume at the minute of rejection. There was no conversation in any case. Template, template, template. Again, a question for recruiters – what's going on?

Third, and most unpleasant: rejections are almost unrelated to the quality of the response. All are either templated or there's silence. Based on this data, it's impossible to understand what I'm doing wrong or why I'm not suitable. Only one thing can be understood: responses into the void.

It should be noted that there is an important nuance in my resume – the desired salary is indicated, and perhaps this is a trigger for rejection, but I didn't check, as I wanted to honestly test the system's performance. Hence, the second assumption – you shouldn't indicate the desired salary in your resume.

What's next

There's a suspicion that for the 3rd or even 4th year now, the Russian IT market has been at rock bottom and there's no demand. Perhaps everyone is in survival mode, but then it's unclear why they publish vacancies. There's also a suspicion that they want to fill leadership positions with meager salaries. I find it hard to believe that the need for IT specialists has suddenly disappeared. Specifically, I will continue to work where I was working, and also come up with various applications for AI tools. In the end, I realized that an agent is a very cool thing that takes over routine tasks, can improve skills and knowledge, but cannot magically solve ordinary problems that are tied to human decisions.

Thank you all who followed my reports, wrote pleasant and unpleasant things too. When I come up with something new, I'll return with a new idea.
