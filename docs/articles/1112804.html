Citibank, the third-largest commercial bank in the US, has updated its terms of service. According to the changes, clients using AI agents will be responsible for resolving any problems caused by them, even if it involves hacking.

"As third-party AI tools become more accessible, it is important for customers to understand the associated risks. Customers who grant third-party AI agents access to their accounts are fully responsible for any actions taken through these agents," a bank representative told TechCrunch.

The bank also warns that if an AI tool causes a data leak or account hacking, the bank will not be responsible for actions that malicious actors take with that account and its linked funds.

In addition to Citibank, several other financial institutions have made similar statements. For example, banking giant Citizens sent letters to clients warning that users are fully responsible for any actions taken by their AI assistants. Last month, JPMorgan and Bank of America, the country's two largest banks, announced a similar policy. Later, PNC, the eighth-largest bank in the US, changed its terms.
