This is not an instruction on how to avoid blocking or how to guarantee account recovery. I'm sharing my personal experience, which might be helpful.

My Claude Pro account was blocked, I mainly used it through Claude Code. The login remained, as did the chat history. But for any active action, it sent a link to the verification portal.

Instructions on "how to pass verification" are sold with the caveat "I managed to do it, you might get lucky": no one has guarantees. I passed the document check, and within a few minutes, I received a block – they returned the money for the dispute instructions, but no one returned the account.

Step-by-step, how it all happened:

1. I used this $20 pro subscription for several months. Yesterday, the session in Claude Code was interrupted with a message that the account could not receive responses, and a link to the verification portal.

2. The same thing in the web interface: some functions are unavailable until verification, active actions are blocked. The chat history, however, remained; it is visible and can be exported.

3. The verification portal asks you to choose who you are acting as (individual or organisation), the type of document (I chose passport), and to continue on your phone via QR code. I couldn't open the link on the same laptop, no matter how I tried; the portal insisted on a different device with a camera. At some point, it broke altogether and said something went wrong, then it revived. Next, it required a photo of my passport and a selfie from my phone.

4. A few minutes later, an email arrived stating that the automated system detected signs of violation of the Supported Countries Policy, and access was revoked.

5. In the web interface, the histories remained accessible, and in the chat window, there was a message about the block and a button for appeal. From the message "your organisation is blocked," I realized that in the last verification attempt, I didn't switch the type from organisation to individual, and it defaulted to organisation. My mistake.

6. Without much hope, I submitted an appeal. I won't provide the exact English text, but it was literally a couple of sentences stating that I use the account for my own research and content creation for personal channels (I didn't provide links to the channels), and that the closure notification didn't specify what exactly I violated, so I don't understand what needs to be corrected. I also mentioned that I would like to continue using the account and am willing to change my approach if something was prohibited.

7. In the morning, I read an email stating that the account was unblocked and another notification about the refund of the remaining subscription balance of ~$5. The account is in Free status, without a subscription, but all the work history is intact.

I want to say that a verification request is not a death sentence.
