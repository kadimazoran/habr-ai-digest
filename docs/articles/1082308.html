Hello everyone, I'm Vladimir Nazarov, founder and head of the search marketing agency Head Promo.

Today I want to talk again about GEO-promotion and neural network responses. The topic has already taken root, many have heard about it and use AI as a separate marketing channel.

But today I'm not talking about how to get into the responses. But about the fact that it's important not only to get into them, but also about how exactly the neural network will talk about the company's brand.

A brand can regularly appear in the responses of ChatGPT, Alice, and Perplexity – and at the same time lose to competitors. A neural network can include a company in a selection, but place it lower, describe it without advantages, or present it as a secondary alternative. Therefore, the visibility indicator alone is not enough: it is necessary to evaluate what I would call the "quality of brand presence in the response."

Let's consider an example with Chinese cars, and ask Perplexity to recommend a reliable Chinese brand:

It gave an exclusive advantage to Chery. At the same time, please note that GAC, FAW, and Jetour brands are also present in the response, but in what context.

"FAW Hongqi is the premium division of FAW, its reliability level is close to Chery (186 PP100), but its model lineup and dealer network may be weaker in some regions."

"GAC Trumpchi is also 186 PP100, known for its crossovers and minivans, but it is less represented in the CIS/Europe than Chery."

In fact, it mentioned these brands, yes. But it didn't "recommend" them. Moreover, it made a direct comparison not in favor of these brands.

Another example, this time from Alice:

How detailed and great "AM Medica" is described, and how meagerly "Kazan Clinic" is. How many advantages of the first option the AI listed: installment payments, rating, number of reviews, different levels, and "the widest selection of check-up programs." And a standard description of the second option, except that the rating and convenient schedule were mentioned.

The same thing: is the brand "Kazan Clinic" represented in the response? Yes. But does it look like the best option? No.

In both examples, the companies made it into the selection, but the neural networks assigned them different roles. One brand became the main recommendation and received detailed argumentation, while the others turned out to be secondary alternatives.

The quality of brand presence in a response can be conditionally divided into several levels:

  • simple mention;

  • presence as a secondary alternative;

  • recommendation with specific arguments;

  • main recommendation;

  • a leading position or benchmark against which others are compared.

A separate situation is when a brand is mentioned with negative caveats, outdated or erroneous facts. The neural network may also confuse the product, region, or conditions, or refer to an irrelevant source.

Therefore, during GEO-monitoring, it is important to evaluate not only the fact of brand presence but also:

  • what positions the brand occupies;

  • what role the neural network assigns to it;

  • which competitors it is mentioned alongside;

  • what advantages explain its selection;

  • whether there are errors or distortions in the responses;

  • what is the tone of the response;

  • what sources are used.

This is the difference between a mature GEO approach and simple mention monitoring.

How to influence the meaning in AI responses?

It's actually not that difficult. The logic is that AI takes company descriptions and advantages from certain resources, based on which they form their response.

Let's take an example:

We have TK Kit, which is described as the largest operator of consolidated cargo, and among its advantages: "fixed tariffs." On the right side of the screenshot, the sources are indicated; we follow them and look for information. We find it on Adpass:

Exactly the same information about specialization in consolidated cargo and emphasis on the advantage of a fixed price.

That is, our task is to convey precisely those advantages, to place those accents that we would like the neural network to emphasize in its responses.

It's good if your company is small and there isn't much information overall. But what if it's a large company, on the market for many years? And who knows what has changed in its positioning over the years. Suppose you have an advertising agency that specialized in working with international brands and promoting in EU countries. Not a great positioning for a Russian agency after 2022.

But you used to write this everywhere, and neural networks will continue to convey it in their responses. Go and systematically correct the information everywhere you can reach: maps, directories, catalogs, profiles on review sites, expert articles, and so on. At the same time, update the relevance and change the dates. In parallel, create new materials, register on new platforms.

And here we are moving not only into the technical process of getting into responses but also into marketing intricacies: study what AI says about your competitors and how to highlight your strengths against this background.

For example:

  • Competitor: wide network of branches across Russia, 2-year warranty, prices from 3000 rubles

  • You: Regional company with a high share of coverage in a specific region, 2.5-year warranty, prices from 2500 rubles, work without days off, installment payments.

To summarize, the plan is as follows:

  • Record how neural networks currently describe the brand.

  • Compare this description with competitors and the company's actual positioning.

  • Identify the meanings and advantages that need to be strengthened.

  • Find sources of outdated or distorted information.

  • Update the website, listings, catalogs, reviews, and external materials.

  • Repeat the measurement and check for changes in responses.

Conclusion

The appearance of a brand in neural network responses is a basic task. The next stage is working on the quality of presence: what role the neural network assigns to the company, what advantages explain its choice, how it compares with competitors, and how accurately it conveys information.

Therefore, in GEO, it is important not only that the neural network names the company but also that after its response, the user has reasons to choose it.
