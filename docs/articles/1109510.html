On November 20-21, 2026, the Skolkovo Media Technologies Cluster will host Prompt Battle: a student AI hackathon as part of the Neurofest festival, which is dedicated to culture, technology, and artificial intelligence and is supported by the Administration of the President of the Russian Federation and the Ministry of Culture of the Russian Federation.

Format

  • 40 students from universities and colleges, 8 teams of 5 people (4 teams per day).

  • Cases from museums, libraries, and theaters.

  • Within a limited time, the team defines the audience, devises a solution, and creates a ready-made artifact: a landing page, a video clip, an advertising campaign, or a visual concept.

  • At the end of the day, there will be a pitching session and defense of the solution before the jury, followed by a final joint defense by the teams and awarding of the winners.

Tools

Technological partner GPTunneL provides participants with access to the Grom lineup and other neural networks of the service. Registration on the platform is possible via Yandex, VK, SMS code, or email (login and password will be needed directly at the venue). Beforehand, it is advisable to check that your account has access to the Grom models.

Program

November 20

  • 11:00–12:10 — Opening and master classes

  • 12:10–12:30 — Cases and curators

  • 12:30–13:30 — Sprint 1: from problem to idea

  • 13:30–13:45 — Break

  • 13:45–15:00 — Sprint 2: prototype, checkpoint, and submission of materials

November 21

  • 11:00–12:10 — Opening and master classes

  • 12:10–12:30 — Cases and curators

  • 12:30–13:30 — Sprint 1: from problem to idea

  • 13:30–14:00 — Break

  • 14:00–14:30 — Expert checkpoint

  • 14:30–15:45 — Sprint 2: prototype and submission of materials

The working part on both days concludes by 16:00.

Master Classes

Before the sprints, participants will attend three practical master classes:

  1. Specifics of promoting cultural projects.

  2. Creating a landing page using AI.

  3. Images, video, and music for project promotion.

How to Participate

  • Teams are formed by universities and colleges: 5 people per team, up to 4 teams per day.

  • Each student registers independently on the Neurofest page and indicates "Prompt Battle Participant" in the "Position" field.

  • Before the event: register on GPTunneL and check access to the Grom lineup.
