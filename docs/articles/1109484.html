On November 28, PSB will hold the IT conference "Neuroday: AI, Trends, Networking" in Yaroslavl. Developers, analysts, and architects will discuss which tasks can already be assigned to AI agents, how to verify their work, and in which cases a human should make the decision.

Program:

  • Alexander Bitko, Frontend Developer at PSB, will demonstrate a VS Code extension and explain how an agent that can independently work with code was created from a chat with an LLM. We will also discuss how the Agent Loop works.

  • Victor Goryachkin, Backend Developer at VK, will talk about using AI at different stages of service development – from idea to production. In the talk, we will discuss how to provide context to an agent, divide work into tasks, and verify the results.

  • Danil Kapustin, Architect at HiveTrace Guardrails, will use incident examples to show where automatic checks are insufficient and at which stages of AI system operation it is worth involving a human.

  • At the "Harness Factory" workshop, Andrey Shumakov, Scrum Master at PSB, will invite participants to design a system that manages the work of several AI agents.

The conference will open with a discussion on how AI is changing the work of IT teams. After the talks, you can continue the conversation with speakers and colleagues at the afterparty.
