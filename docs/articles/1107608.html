On November 12, 2026, Anthropic's updated usage policy came into effect, and Russian media reported that Claude was being blocked for companies in Russia. We've compiled all the dates from the past year and checked what's actually new in this story.

Timeline

In May 2026, Claude blocked several hundred accounts from Russia (Anti-Malware, 05/08/2026).

In June, the company warned that it might request documents and selfies: this is how accounts from countries where Claude is unavailable are verified (Vazhnye Istorii, 10/29/2026).

In early October, a second wave of blocking Russian users occurred (Novaya Gazeta Europe, 10/02/2026).

In late October, Claude began requesting passports through the Persona service. Some accounts were blocked immediately after uploading Russian international passports, including those belonging to individuals living in countries where Claude officially operates (Vazhnye Istorii, The Moscow Times, 10/29/2026).

What the Rules Say

According to Meduza's analysis, the update includes a section titled "Clarifying our Supported Regions Policy." Claude cannot be used by individuals physically located in unsupported countries. Companies registered in such countries, along with their employees, are also prohibited from using Claude, even when working from a country where Claude is available. Companies directly or indirectly owned by entities from unsupported countries are also banned (Meduza, 11/11/2026).

Www1.ru adds that Claude Code and Cowork are now listed among the restricted tools, and the restrictions apply to the website, API, and access to models through third-party cloud platforms (www1.ru, 11/12/2026).

What's Not New Here

Meduza compared the country list page with an archived copy from May 12, 2026, and reported that the text had not changed. Russia was not included in the list of supported countries before. According to their assessment, the restrictions have been in place since at least May, and the discussions about a ban specifically on November 12 are exaggerated.

We did not verify the policy text itself against the original source: it is presented based on the media's retelling.

What This Means in Practice

There is no clear picture of exactly why blocks are occurring: Anthropic has not publicly explained it. It is unknown whether blocks are due to citizenship or because users previously accessed Claude from countries where it is not available.

There is only one practical conclusion: if work chats and files are stored in Claude, it is advisable to save them locally in advance, as access to them may be lost after blocking.
