So what should Habr do?

Moderators are unwilling to remove articles that some people believe are AI-generated. Because the opinion of these some people might be mistaken.

They use GigaCheck and Yandex Neurodetector because they are free. And the use of paid, good detectors is likely not within their budget.

For this reason, it's impossible to write a script that would check every article, every post, and every comment with these detectors. Considering the volume of content on Habr, it would cost hundreds of dollars per month.

Let's assume they find such a large sum of money. Then, next to the title of each article, an icon could be added, showing the percentage of AI-generated content. And users could filter the feed so that these articles are not displayed.

But how long have you seen new functionality on Habr? Even if there is money for AI detectors and a desire to implement such functionality, it will either appear very late, or not appear at all.
