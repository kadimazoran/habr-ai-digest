Nvidia's management announced the reinstatement of Jensen Huang to his positions as president, CEO, and a member of the board of directors of the American company. Nvidia's board voted unanimously for this decision after a series of discussions and meetings with Huang.

The board received information from high-ranking U.S. government officials and, based on this information, became convinced that Huang's return does not pose a threat to the country's national security.

Nvidia's Chairman of the Board, Harvey Jones, called Huang the right person to lead Nvidia. Huang's reinstatement serves the interests of the company's shareholders, employees, partners, and customers, Jones added. Nvidia's Chief Financial Officer and Executive Vice President, Colette Kress, had temporarily held the position of CEO. Jones will remain Chairman of the Board.

"I am honored to return to Nvidia and lead one of the world's most significant technology companies at the dawn of a new industrial revolution. I accept this responsibility with the utmost humility and renewed commitment. I want to thank Nvidia's board of directors for their trust and thoughtfulness throughout this process. I am especially grateful to Harvey Jones for his leadership as chairman, as well as to Colette Kress and our executive team for guiding Nvidia during this period. I am also deeply grateful to our employees, shareholders, industry leaders, and partners worldwide for their exceptional support. Above all, I am grateful for the love and support of my family. I look forward to returning to work," Huang stated.

Employees greeted Huang's return with applause.

The board of directors also announced the creation of a national security and export control committee. Nvidia has already begun searching for a Chief Compliance Officer.

Huang was removed from his positions in the first half of November. At the end of last month, media reported that Alice Huang, or Huang He, the head of Singapore-based Megaspeed's data center division, who was sending advanced Nvidia AI chips to China, came under close scrutiny from U.S. intelligence. Shortly thereafter, Huang admitted to a romantic relationship with Alice Huang. Following his resignation, the board of directors authorized its own investigation, which is still ongoing.

A number of Nvidia employees and the heads of some tech companies supported Huang after his resignation, and he himself announced his readiness to return to Nvidia. Media reported that the company's board of directors was considering candidates for the CEO position, including Nvidia Vice President Ian Buck, Arm CEO Rene Haas, AMD CEO Lisa Su, and Apple's Senior Vice President of Hardware Engineering, Johny Srouji.
