What You'll Need for a ChatGPT Bot on VKontakte

The list is short:

  • A GPTunneL account with a positive balance;

  • A VKontakte community where you have administrator rights. The "Work with API" section requires specific permissions, so check your privileges beforehand.

Don't have a community yet? Go to "Communities" → "Create Community". The type and theme of the community don't matter for the bot; it will happily operate in a sneaker store or a cactus enthusiast club.
