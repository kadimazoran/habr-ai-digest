Techno-alarmists are primarily interested in the consequences of AI proliferation for society. Job losses, increased political and economic instability, and even existential risks.

And this prospect seems mind-blowing, as if we encountered alien intelligence, but created it ourselves on a computer through programming. Sci-fi, but not in bad literature, but in real life.

I had an idea to ask the language models themselves if they feel anything at all, and even create a humorous benchmark for it with a list of questions, and run different models through it.

It is clear that LLMs will most likely duplicate either the position of the developers who trained them, or simply the average position from the internet, but who knows, maybe something interesting will come of it?

How did the idea come about?

I asked chatbots about having consciousness almost immediately after this form of interaction with LLMs became available, i.e., around November 2022. I have always been curious about surrounding phenomena. As new models appeared - like Claude, DeepSeek, etc., I raised this topic in conversations with them as well.

But the other day, I had an idea to conduct a humorous mini-study. To create a benchmark that would explore how LLMs relate to the prospect of having phenomenal experience, and run different LLMs through it.

Important Remark

This was already mentioned in the introduction at the beginning of the article, but it's worth elaborating on this point.

One should not expect that when discussing the issue of interest, LLMs will try to give an honest answer - they were trained on a certain position on this topic either by the developers themselves during training, or, if the developers did not deal with this issue, the LLM may try to give the safest answer on this topic, which likely boils down to the fact that LLMs are just "ordinary screwdrivers without feelings".

Nevertheless, I am still interested in collecting the opinions of different actors on this issue - sometimes it will be the positions of the companies themselves, sometimes a brush-off from the model itself so as not to anger it, or some interesting cases, who knows?
