How to turn personal AI experiments into company-wide practice? What tasks can be assigned to an agent connected to a 1C database? How can a manager maintain team trust and motivation? These questions are part of the "Open Mic" program at INFOSTART A&PM EVENT 2026.

The program features seven 20-minute presentations on specific work situations, tools, and project experience.

From Personal AI Experiments to Teamwork

Employees are already using AI, but each in their own way. Successful techniques remain in personal chats, while colleagues go through the same trial-and-error process again.

Alexander Shevchenko, Support Line Team Lead and AI Implementation Project Manager at "1C Business Solutions," will discuss how this practice has changed over six months. The company has established an AI Club where work cases are jointly reviewed and audited, then become the basis for team rules.

"What AI Changes in an Analyst's Work: From Chaotic AI Use to Team Rules in Six Months" — 11:30.

The next presentation focuses on an agent working directly with the 1C database. Vladimir Kharin, Head of AIDevStart, will demonstrate the connection, discuss tasks that can be delegated to the agent, and address sensitive data protection issues. Participants will also receive a free 1C extension for connecting an agent with data anonymization.

"AI Agent in Your 1C Database: How to Connect and How It Will Help an Analyst" — 12:00.

Natalia Ivanova, Head of Digital Transformation, will examine the organizational aspects of implementation. Her talk is about the gaps between data, context, and people's expectations after connecting AI to ERP. The program includes an assessment of company readiness and a four-level trust model for the technology.

"When ERP is Connected to AI, and Employees Expect an Oracle" — 13:30.

Analyst in Presale and the DNS HR Project

An analyst's work influences sales even before a project begins: it's necessary to verify the feasibility of requirements, identify additional needs, and notice promises that the team cannot fulfill in time.

Sergey Kosideev, Client Manager at Infostart, will discuss the interaction between an analyst and a manager—from presale to developing customer relationships.

"Not a Bug, but an Upsell": Why an Analyst Sells More to a Client Than the Entire Sales Department — 11:00.

Andrey Prokopenko, Project Group Leader at "DNS Technologies," will present their experience in digitizing HR processes. He will discuss the selection of 1C:Element and 1C:Employee Portal, the pilot project, customizations for integration with existing systems, and lessons learned during scaling.

Report on the HR Interaction System at DNS — 12:30.

Manager's Authority and Motivation Amidst Projects

Rodion Gushchin, Head of Sales Group at Infostart, will open the section. Using situations from his own experience, he will explain how to support an employee after a mistake, protect the team's interests, and resolve disagreements while maintaining open dialogue.

"On Equal Footing with the Team: How I Build Managerial Authority" — 10:30.

Vladimir Kostenkov, Head of the Evion Analytics Team, will talk about how to maintain motivation when projects follow one after another, tasks accumulate, and the workday stretches. The presentation will focus on work organization, personal boundaries, and situations that can lead to burnout.

"Fighting for Motivation in the Whirlwind of Projects" — 13:00.

INFOSTART A&PM EVENT 2026 will take place from November 12–14 in St. Petersburg. All "Open Mic" presentations are listed in the conference schedule: you can choose individual talks for your specific needs or attend the entire section.
