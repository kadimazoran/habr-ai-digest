Error During MP4 Assembly

During video assembly, the system's FFmpeg failed to launch: it was missing the libvpx.11.dylib library. By this point, 120 PNGs were already ready. Codex verified that all files existed and had non-zero sizes, and used Blender's built-in H.264 encoder to create the MP4.

This resulted in the file desk-lamp-orbit.mp4. The separately saved frames were useful in case of failure: work could be continued from video assembly. To replicate this method, your Blender build must support H.264 export.
