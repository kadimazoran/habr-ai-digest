My name is Denis, I've been in IT for 22 years: I've gone from developer to development team lead. For many years, I've been building teams, mentoring people, and implementing automation, including AI-based solutions. Therefore, when my wife, an ICF coach and system analyst with 18 years of IT experience, showed me how much time "helping" professionals spend on everything surrounding the session itself, I immediately recognized a familiar pattern – what I encounter daily in my work and try to automate: too many tools where one is needed. That's how we started building 121ai together.

One session, five places

To conduct one session and not lose anything, you need to open a dialer, turn on a voice recorder, take notes, then transcribe the recording somewhere, and update the client list. Each tab seems like a small thing, but together they take away attention and time that could be spent on the client or on yourself.

This is not just a problem for coaches. Psychologists, mentors, consultants, tutors, and anyone who works one-on-one face a similar set of tools.

What we've done

121ai is a single dashboard where all of this is gathered:

  • client cards with meeting history;

  • a calendar and a call via a link: the client doesn't need an app or registration, they join from their browser;

  • meeting recording only with client consent, only audio is recorded;

  • transcription by roles and meeting summaries that immediately appear in the client's card.

How it looks in practice

Four steps: add a client, schedule a meeting, the client receives an email with a link, and after the meeting, the transcription and summary are already ready in their card. Nothing needs to be manually compiled for the next session.

A protocol that's convenient to use

The transcription can be exported to PDF or DOCX. The format is suitable for certification commissions, and it's also convenient to use this document to prepare for assessments, supervision, or mentoring.

One of our first users told us that preparing a post-session report for a client took her a minute, whereas before it took her 10–15 minutes. We're doing all this for those minutes.

About trust

It's important for us that the client feels at ease. Recording doesn't start automatically: the client confirms consent before joining and sees an on-screen notification when recording is active. Audio is deleted after approximately three hours, while the transcription and summaries remain in the client's card.
