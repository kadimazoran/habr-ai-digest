Business proposed allowing independent laboratories to test AI systems in testbeds, i.e., in special environments. This will help customers and information system owners assess the possible risks of using neural networks and make a decision on implementation. If the AI system passes the test, is capable of performing certain tasks, and complies with security requirements, it acquires the status of trusted. This is stated in the draft concept of trust in AI systems. Vedomosti familiarized themselves with the project, its authenticity was confirmed by two sources: one in an IT company, the second in the Ministry of Digital Development.

The concept is based on the project of the Consortium for Research on Security of Artificial Intelligence Technologies, said a representative of its administrator - the Foundation for the Promotion of Secure Information Technologies (FSRBIT). According to him, representatives of organizations participating in the consortium constituted more than half of the relevant working group.

A testbed is an environment for reproducing the conditions of the system's operation and simulating threats, according to the definition in the document. The laboratory conducts agreed-upon tests in this environment and issues a conclusion on confirmed properties and limitations. According to the document, AI agents will be checked for access rights, resistance to changes in instructions, and the ability to undo actions.

The status of the testbed operator and the legal significance of the conclusions will be determined separately. Until then, it will be considered a "technical document on the results of the check." A representative of the Ministry of Digital Development explained to Vedomosti that the concept is recommendatory and does not introduce new mandatory procedures for admission, testing, or conformity assessment.

In August, Vedomosti reported on plans to involve independent laboratories in checking sovereign and national AI models in Russia for compliance with "traditional spiritual and moral values."

Trusted AI is not necessarily domestic. A national AI model is created by a Russian developer who controls its main characteristics but may use foreign components under an open license. The most stringent requirements are set for a sovereign AI model - the developer must train the model from scratch without using foreign components.

A positive conclusion from the laboratory confirms the system's properties only within the scope of the conducted tests and does not guarantee its safety throughout the entire period of use, explained the FSRBIT representative. The testing organization, according to him, is responsible for compliance with methodologies and the reliability of the conclusion, while the system owner is responsible for the decision on its application, operating conditions, and management of residual risks.

The check does not exempt the developer, integrator, and information system owner from responsibility for possible incidents, agrees Ilya Zharsky, managing partner of the Veta expert group. To recover damages from the laboratory, it will be necessary to prove its violation and its connection with the damage.
