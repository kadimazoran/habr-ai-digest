Simplity, a provider of IT and cybersecurity services and solutions, and Diakont, Russia's largest software developer for the financial sector and other economic industries, have signed a technological partnership agreement.

The collaboration will allow customers to use Diakont's low-code development ecosystem Digital Q and the "Data Factory" solution (Digital Q.DataFactory) as a SaaS service within a secure, fault-tolerant cloud infrastructure. Diakont provides software solutions as a service, while Simplity ensures deployment and technical support based on the OXYGEN cloud infrastructure.

The companies announced the partnership at the "DBMS Day 2026" conference, which Diakont hosted on October 27.

What the partnership offers

Launching corporate data platforms often begins with addressing infrastructure tasks: purchasing hardware, configuring software, and organizing its support. This requires time, money, and additional specialists.

The Simplity and Diakont partnership simplifies this process. Companies will be able to launch projects in days instead of weeks, without significant upfront investment in hardware, flexibly scale computing power, and delegate infrastructure tasks to a professional team.

A separate area of cooperation is joint piloting. This will allow not only evaluating the capabilities of Diakont's products but also testing them under load, selecting the necessary infrastructure, and preparing for further project development.

For working with sensitive data, Simplity can provide a secure, fault-tolerant cloud infrastructure. The company has its own team of information security specialists and a Class A SOC (State SOC) GosSOPKA. This allows delegating the implementation of necessary security measures, vulnerability remediation, security monitoring, and compliance with regulatory requirements to Simplity.

Customer Convenience

Dmitry Turpitko, Head of Strategic Customer Relations at Simplity: "Not everyone wants to build and maintain their own data infrastructure. You can delegate these tasks to Simplity, and together with Diakont, we will provide you with the "Data Factory" as a service."

"For industrial application of artificial intelligence, it is not enough to simply gather data in one place. It is necessary to ensure its quality, security, and controlled access. Thanks to the partnership with Simplity, customers will be able to use the capabilities of the "Data Factory" in a ready-made cloud infrastructure, reducing the time for preparing the technological environment and focusing on solving business tasks," commented Ilya Shuykov, Head of the "Data Factory" solution at Diakont.

"Data Factory" is designed for integrating, storing, and processing corporate data, building analytics, machine learning, and developing solutions using artificial intelligence. It automates data work, from collection to obtaining analytical results.

Special attention in "Data Factory" is paid to the secure application of AI. The solution provides access control, personal data protection, and monitoring of user and AI agent interactions with language models.

Digital Q.DataFactory is part of the Digital Q low-code development ecosystem.
