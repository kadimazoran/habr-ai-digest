On September 22nd at 16:00 MSK, we will hold an open online podcast about implementing GenAI in insurance.

Together with Anton Isanin, Technical Director of AlfaStrakhovanie, we will discuss what happens after the first experiments with AI: how to choose viable scenarios, integrate them into real processes, and achieve measurable results.

We will discuss:

  • how to understand when an AI pilot is ready for industrial use;

  • where GenAI is already helping with sales, claims settlement, contact centers, and agents;

  • which solutions can be entrusted to an algorithm, and where a human should remain involved;

  • how to organize corporate knowledge, access rights, and secure work with LLMs;

  • by what metrics to evaluate the results of implementation.

Participants in the podcast:

Anton Isanin — Technical Director of AlfaStrakhovanie

Sergey Kozhemyakin — Executive Director of AGIMA and host of the meeting

The broadcast will be useful for technical leaders, developers, architects, product teams, and specialists who are already testing GenAI or choosing scenarios for its application.
