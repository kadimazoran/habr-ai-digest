In recent years, neural networks have not only made a giant leap in quality but have also spread widely. The Western market is represented primarily by ChatGPT, in Russia Alice and GigaCHAT are trying to compete, and from China's "national team," the free and quite powerful DeepSeek is the main representative...

But is it really the only one? Well... not really. In fact, there is a real boom in large language models in China right now, and many of them not only compete with ChatGPT but also try to surpass it in a number of specific tasks.

In this material, we will look at several good and interesting representatives.

❯ MiniMax. LLM from the creators of Genshin Impact

Each of you who uses, for example, TikTok, has at least once seen the logo of this neural network. In the field of video generation, MiniMax is considered one of the leaders, competing on equal terms with Runway Gen-3 and OpenAI's Sora.

The company's flagship product is currently MiniMax-H3, which understands text, images, other videos, and audio simultaneously. It can do a lot, from the basic text-to-video to a director format, where you give the neural network the first and last frame of a clip, and it invents what's in between.

What do the creators of "Genshin" have to do with this? Well, they invested in other MiniMax modules called MiniMax Speech and MiniMax-M3.1. The first is an advanced speech synthesis and cloning module. It is trained to reproduce natural human emotions, sighs, laughter, and intonation changes depending on the context of the situation. For miHoYo, for example, this is a foundation for the future, allowing them to generate thousands of terabytes of high-quality audio dubbing for secondary quests on the fly, without the need to keep actors in recording studios for years.

The second module is the company's agent text model with a gigantic context window of 1 million tokens and the ability to use tools autonomously.

❯ The foundation for many. Qwen by Alibaba

The LLM industry can be very roughly and conditionally divided into two, so to speak, camps. The first is accustomed to focusing on closed models like ChatGPT or Claude, the classic of capitalism, so to speak. And then there's Alibaba (yes, the same AliExpress), waving its hand with its Qwen, on which startups and corporations worldwide build their solutions.

The point is that Alibaba consistently adheres to the strategy of AI democratization. While others close their source code, Alibaba releases the weights of its advanced neural networks into the public domain on GitHub and Hugging Face. Any company can download it, fine-tune it on its own data, and use it with complete confidentiality on its own servers.

The freedom doesn't end there. Qwen has several sizes for deployment depending on the user's capabilities. Qwen 2.5 Coder 32B is particularly popular, which in terms of code writing quality has come very close to the closed GPT-4o, but runs locally on graphics cards like the RTX 3080/4090.

And of course, not only text models are released under the Qwen brand. Advanced Vision models Qwen-VL / Qwen-Image (image recognition and editing by pixels), as well as Qwen-Audio (sound understanding) and multimodal AI agents Qwen-Omni, capable of processing video, audio, and text simultaneously in real-time, are built on the same architecture.

You can try all of this yourself for free on their website, or download the distribution from GitHub and Hugging Face and deploy it locally.

❯ Ernie Bot by Baidu. The answer to ChatGPT from China's Google equivalent

You've also noticed that over the past couple of years, we've started searching for information on Google a bit differently thanks to the neural network integrated into it, right? Well, in China, there is, firstly, its own answer to Google called Baidu (which holds over 75% of the PRC's search market and possesses powerful infrastructure), and secondly, on its infrastructure, there is its own neural network that is trying very hard to compete with ChatGPT?

As of today, Ernie Bot's audience has exceeded 300 million users, and the model itself has evolved from an uncertain start to a full-fledged multimodal flagship. The most advanced version to date, Ernie 5.1 Turbo, was trained on billions of Baidu search queries. In benchmarks in Chinese (context understanding, logic, knowledge of local culture and laws), it shows results comparable to GPT-4o.

Among other things, Ernie is deeply integrated into the Baidu search engine itself. Any business or user can create an autonomous AI agent based on Ernie to automate routine tasks (customer support, content generation) without a single line of code. Ernie technologies are also integrated into Apollo, Baidu's autonomous driving system, which manages China's largest fleet of robotaxis.

There's just one nuance: this is a story deeply for the domestic market, which means Ernie Bot strictly adheres to state regulatory norms. You won't get an answer to the question of what happened in Tiananmen Square; you can't even try.

❯ Seedance by the creators of TikTok. An LLM that turned the world upside down

It's actually difficult to talk about Seedance as a tool without mentioning the context of what a phenomenon it is.

In mid-February 2026, the renowned Irish director and Oscar nominee Ruairi Robinson published a 15-second clip depicting a very realistic and fierce fight between characters resembling Tom Cruise and Brad Pitt. It all looked like leaked footage from a film in production (if you don't turn on the sound and don't listen to what they're saying, which includes jokes about Epstein), or a snippet from a music video.

But no, the video was made in Seedance 2.0 by ByteDance using a text prompt of just two sentences. The result panicked many Hollywood figures and provoked them to start scolding the company that created the neural network.

Disney and Paramount Skydance even accused the Chinese holding company of a "virtual armed robbery" of their intellectual property. Due to the general scandal, the Seedance 2.5 update received a powerful AI moderator. Now, the system automatically blocks 100% of attempts to upload copyrighted images and filters out about 80% of text prompts attempting to recreate Hollywood stars or film franchises.

Well, yes, in essence, Seedance 2.5 supports up to 50 multimodal references simultaneously (images, sounds, movements) and produces cinematic quality video clips up to 4K resolution (depending on the service using Seedance, some have a 1080p limit) up to 30 seconds long using a complex multimodal diffusion architecture.

And it was this that turned the world of memes, short videos, and even advertising upside down. That's all for now. Have a good week!
