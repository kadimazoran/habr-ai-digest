Why a Team Needs Business, Not Individual Plus Plans

The main difference is not in features, but in data. On Plus, conversations can be used for model training by default: disabling this is a user setting that an employee can switch back at any time. On Business, opting out of training is a contractual guarantee from OpenAI that cannot be overridden by a checkbox. For companies where chat involves client data, finances, or trade secrets, this is a fundamental difference – and often the only option that passes information security approval.

Next, management: a shared workspace with common GPTs and projects, an admin console with usage statistics, SAML SSO and domain verification, SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO/IEC 27001 certifications, and integration with corporate applications via connectors. Plus, legal compliance: OpenAI's terms of service prohibit sharing access to personal accounts with others – having "one Plus for the department" violates the terms and risks account suspension.

Finally, the price: with annual payment, a seat in Business costs the same as an individual Plus ($20), but includes all the listed benefits. The minimum is 2 seats, meaning an entry cost of $480 per year.

Why Direct Payment Isn't Possible

There are two reasons, both on OpenAI's side. The first is region: Russia is not among the supported countries, and the service's terms of service explicitly warn of account blocking for access and payment methods from unsupported regions. The second is the payment method: Business is a self-serve product strictly requiring card payment. Invoices, bank transfers, or deferred payments are not available for this plan; contract-based billing only applies to Enterprise.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does ChatGPT Business differ from Plus?

In three ways: a contractual guarantee that team conversations are not used for model training (on Plus, this is just a user setting), a shared workspace with an admin console, and SAML SSO. With annual payment, a seat costs the same as Plus – $20 per month, so for a team of two or more, Business is more advantageous overall.

Are ChatGPT Team and ChatGPT Business the same thing?

Yes. On August 29, 2025, OpenAI renamed Team to Business – features, prices, and limits remained the same, and existing subscribers were migrated automatically. If an article compares Team as a separate plan alongside Business, it is outdated.

Can I pay for ChatGPT Business with a Russian bank card?

No. Russia is not on OpenAI's list of supported countries, the payment gateway rejects Russian bank cards, and the service's terms of service explicitly warn of account blocking for access and payment methods from unsupported regions.

Can I get an invoice or bill from OpenAI for accounting?

Not on the Business plan – it's a self-serve subscription with card payment only, no invoices or bank transfers. Contract-based billing is only available for Enterprise with an annual contract. Therefore, Russian legal entities receive closing documents from a Russian service provider who handles the payment.

Does OpenAI use our company's data for training?

Not on the Business and Enterprise plans: this is stipulated in the terms and does not depend on user settings. On Free, Go, Plus, and Pro, training is enabled by default and can only be disabled manually in data settings.

Is the subscription set up for our company's account?

Yes. We pay for Business for your company's workspace: employees, chat history, and shared GPTs remain with you, and passwords and access are not transferred to third parties.

We need an API for our product, not a chat interface. Is this subscription suitable?
