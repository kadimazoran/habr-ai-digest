Nvidia announced the resignation of Jensen Huang from his positions as President, CEO, and member of the Board of Directors. The current Vice President and CFO of the American company, Colette Kress, will temporarily serve as CEO until a permanent successor is appointed. The Board of Directors will consider internal and external candidates for the new CEO position.

At the end of last month, media reported on an investigation into a spy conspiracy against Huang. Alice Huang, or Huang He, the head of the Singaporean company Megaspeed in the data center sector, came under the close scrutiny of American intelligence services. US authorities established that she was an agent of Chinese intelligence and was transmitting information to Beijing.

This month, 63-year-old Huang admitted to a romantic relationship with Alice Huang, apologizing for his actions to his family and colleagues. The Nvidia Board of Directors announced an independent investigation. Later, media reported on another extramarital affair of Huang with one of his subordinates at Nvidia. The Chinese Ministry of Foreign Affairs denies accusations that Alice Huang worked for the country's intelligence.

Kress has held the position of Executive Vice President and CFO of Nvidia since joining the company in the fall of 2013. Prior to that, she worked for nearly 25 years in the financial departments of Cisco and Microsoft. Huang chose Kress from 22 candidates; when asked about the contract duration, the head of Nvidia replied, "As long as we are both alive." According to Kress, her sons, who are gamers, encouraged her to join Nvidia because the company produces video cards. However, even then, Huang saw the company's future in AI. In 2013, Nvidia's value did not exceed $10 billion.

Harvey S. Jones has been appointed the first-ever Chairman of Nvidia's Board of Directors. Nvidia announced that the CEO change will not affect operational activities, customer commitments, or the product roadmap. The report for the third quarter of fiscal year 2027 will be released on November 17.

In the spring of last year, the American fast-food chain Denny's introduced a "genius breakfast" called Nvidia Breakfast Bytes, costing $5. The breakfast, with an energy value of 520 calories, includes four pork sausages, four pancakes, and a portion of maple syrup, as well as instructions on how to wrap a sausage in a pancake. This is roughly what Huang did when Nvidia was just starting out.

Huang founded Nvidia in 1993 at the age of 30. The company contributed to the global artificial intelligence boom. Nvidia has repeatedly topped the list of the most valuable companies in the world. By the end of October 2026, Nvidia's capitalization was approximately $4.1 trillion, but amid the scandal, its value dropped to $3.86 trillion. Huang owns 3.6% of Nvidia. Approximately 60 people were directly subordinate to him.
