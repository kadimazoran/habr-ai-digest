Let's Clarify a Few Things

NodaLogic is a completely free, non-cloud platform. You can download it from GitHub. and deploy it yourself. The server, your developed configurations, and data – everything resides and runs on your infrastructure. (You can also use nmaker.pw, but it's better to keep production databases on your own infrastructure). I wrote about it here: https://habr.com/ru/articles/1011090/ No hidden subscriptions or anything similar – complete ownership, open license.

And if you decide to develop a solution for your business, you have two paths to choose from:

  1. You can do it yourself: review the documentation and videos, download examples, go to nmaker.pw to use the standard configurator or deploy it yourself and create a configuration, and then use the resulting configuration. I position NodaLogic as a low-code platform and fiercely advocate for it to be truly low-code, not a sham. Before this, I spent 7 years developing SimpleUI, which many find simple, but I decided to prove that NL is even simpler and more convenient. In short, it's a good path, I recommend it. Existing LLMs can help you, and many, as I know, are actively writing configurations for NL using ChatGPT and similar tools. This is a free option.

  2. Alternatively, you can go the route of a commercial service, which this article is dedicated to – N-Reactor. It will do everything turnkey according to strict NodaLogic methodology, architecture, and performance standards, but for a fee. So, what's all this about? I just want to clarify that as a result of interacting with the service, you will receive the same configuration as in the first option (literally a .nod file), only AI and sophisticated algorithms will do everything for you. Here's how it works: the pipeline releases the configuration into a cloud deployment, you test it, onboard users, deploy it on your device, conduct load tests or other tests, and ONLY after confirming it meets your needs, you pay and download the .nod file. It's somewhat like a nursery where I grow not thuya trees, but custom business solutions for you. More precisely, it's not me, but the model and smart algorithms that do it. I call this a 'generated product' (A generated product is not a standard configuration that is later customized. It is a new configuration created for a specific business, but generated according to the standards and patterns of a standard product).
