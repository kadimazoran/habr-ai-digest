3) Golden Leads: Raising the threshold to 95% and new alert logic
Every day, I receive "Golden Vacancies" to which, in theory, I should pay special attention (find founders, HRDs, CPOs... and other key decision-makers), which will help me get into a company, bypassing typical AI HR scoring. The vacancies are really good, but if I process each of them like this, then job searching will turn into a job for me. This doesn't suit me, so I will break this issue down into two stages of implementation:

4) Integrated my availability calendar into HHH.
Last Monday, a call came from an unknown number. On the line, a cheerful voice: "Anatoly, good afternoon! This is regarding the vacancy at ***. Let's continue...". At that moment, my AI assistant in the HR chat showed excessive independence: it cheerfully confirmed the call for me without asking my consent. Consequently, HR calls me to continue the initiated dialogue, and I don't know what company it is about, what we discussed, etc. Fortunately, HR carefully studied my website and the HHHunter project before the call. I openly explained to him that my AI agent was negotiating with him, testing hypotheses of product hiring. HR appreciated the boldness of the experiment, and we had an excellent and equal conversation about the real situation in the HR market, the overload of recruiters, and the bottlenecks in hiring. P.S. The vacancy itself turned out to be good. In my city, to move software products from 0 to 1, while first finding "Segment A". The HR person turned out to be an IT director with very good brains. We had a concise and open conversation. He wasn't scared that I'm not a corporate product person. We agreed to meet with him and the company owner this week.

Engineering fix: To avoid such surprises, I created a unified booking page based on Google Calendar and linked it to the bot:

  • If HR immediately suggests a specific time: the bot checks my actual slots via API. If the slot is free, the bot tentatively books the time, adds the meeting to the calendar with all the vacancy details, and sends me an alert.

  • If coordination begins: the bot sends a link to the booking page, where the interlocutor can choose a convenient slot in one click.

The main rule: the final confirmation of the meeting remains strictly with me. Now, you can schedule an introductory meeting both through the bot and through the calendar interface, but under my personal control.
