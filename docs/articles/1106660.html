OpenAI announced that on December 11, 2026, it will completely cease support for Advanced Voice Mode and Standard Voice Mode in ChatGPT. The company plans to phase out outdated voice solutions and focus all efforts on developing GPT-Live.

In 2024, OpenAI introduced Advanced Voice Mode, which allowed users to engage in natural language conversations with ChatGPT. Unlike the initial Standard Voice Mode, which relied on separate models for speech recognition, language processing, and speech synthesis, Advanced Voice Mode could process and generate audio directly. This resulted in faster responses and made interactions more natural. However, both modes—Standard and Advanced—operated sequentially: the model had to wait for the user to finish speaking before responding.

In July of this year, OpenAI introduced GPT-Live, a new voice model utilizing a full-duplex architecture. This allows ChatGPT to listen and speak simultaneously, making conversations more fluid and dynamic. Furthermore, GPT-Live can handle complex queries by routing tasks to OpenAI's more powerful text models without interrupting the ongoing conversation.

According to OpenAI, since the launch of GPT-Live in July, the use of voice communication in ChatGPT has nearly doubled, and most users interacting with the service have already transitioned to GPT-Live.

On November 10, the company began notifying users about the discontinuation of these modes. Starting December 11, both will be removed from the Voice settings section. Users who had one of these modes selected will be automatically switched to GPT-Live.

Past conversation history will be preserved in ChatGPT; these can be continued in text format or using GPT-Live.

Real-time video streaming and screen sharing features in ChatGPT will continue to use Advanced Voice Mode, as GPT-Live does not yet support these capabilities. Voice communication with custom ChatGPT versions (Custom GPTs) will also be handled through Advanced Voice Mode until December 11.

OpenAI's decision to abandon the previous voice modes has been met with negative reactions from some ChatGPT users. Users note that in Standard Voice mode, responses are more detailed, and the conversation maintains better coherence, whereas GPT-Live often interrupts, loses context, or provides less useful answers. Some paid subscribers have even threatened to cancel their subscriptions if OpenAI does not reconsider its decision.

OpenAI has acknowledged that some users may prefer the conversational style characteristic of Advanced Voice or Standard Voice modes. The company reports that it is working on improving GPT-Live, specifically aiming to make responses more in-depth, ensure better conversational coherence, and expand personalization capabilities, drawing on the experience gained from previous versions.
