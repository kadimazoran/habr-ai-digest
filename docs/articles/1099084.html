When an entrepreneur asks how much an AI bot for a website or messenger costs, they usually get two answers. The developer names the development price, and about the monthly costs for the neural network itself, they say "pennies." Both answers are incomplete. Development is paid once, and the model charges for each response to the client, and after six months, this budget line can become noticeable.

Let's calculate the monthly costs for the model using the open prices of two Russian platforms, GigaChat from Sber and Yandex AI Studio, in October 2026. Without formulas or code, using the example of a typical support bot.

Why Russian models

For businesses in Russia, GigaChat and Yandex models have two practical advantages. Client data is processed on servers in Russia, which simplifies discussions about personal data and Federal Law No. 152-FZ. Payment is made in rubles via contract and invoice, without foreign cards or intermediaries. The quality of responses varies between models, but for a typical support bot that answers based on a company's knowledge base, both platforms fulfill the task.

What you pay for

Models are priced per token. A token is a piece of text, usually part of a word. You pay for both what you send to the model and what it responds. The main surprise for owners is that much more is sent than just the client's question.

For the bot to respond relevantly, each request to the model includes instructions on how to behave, relevant snippets from the company's knowledge base, and the last few turns of the conversation. A question like "How much is delivery to Kazan?" takes about ten tokens, while the entire request is around three thousand. The bot's response adds a few hundred more.

Calculating with an example

Let's take the support bot for an online store. The request to the model is about 3,000 tokens, and the response is about 400. Then, one response to a client costs approximately 0.22 ₽ on GigaChat Lite, 0.38 ₽ on Alice AI LLM Flash, 0.68 ₽ on YandexGPT Lite, 1.70 ₽ on GigaChat Pro, 1.98 ₽ on Alice AI LLM, 2.21 ₽ on GigaChat Max, and 2.72 ₽ on YandexGPT Pro 5.1.

The rest depends on the number of responses. A small store with a thousand responses per month will spend from 220 ₽ to 2,700 ₽ on the model, and the difference between models is almost negligible. A company with 10,000 responses per month will pay from 2,200 ₽ on the junior GigaChat model to 27,200 ₽ on the senior YandexGPT model. With 100,000 responses, the difference between the junior and senior models can reach 250,000 ₽ per month, making the model choice a financial decision.

These figures are approximate. Models tokenize text differently, and the same question results in a different number of tokens for each. The exact cost is shown by the platform itself after the first few weeks of operation, so it's advisable to budget for the model with a buffer and review it after a month.

What else besides the model

The model is just one line of expense. The bot needs to be developed and connected to the website, messengers, and CRM once, its knowledge base populated, and it needs to be trained to transfer complex questions to a live manager. Then, the knowledge base requires updates: prices, delivery terms, or assortment change. If this is not done, the bot will confidently provide outdated information, and paying for such responses is the most frustrating.

A separate expense item is quality control. Someone from the staff must review a sample of dialogues weekly and note errors. Without this, you will learn about the bot's problems from dissatisfied customers.

How to choose a model

For a bot that answers typical questions about delivery, payment, availability, and appointments, junior models like GigaChat Lite or Alice AI LLM Flash are usually sufficient. Senior models are needed when the bot needs to reason: compare options, select products based on a task description, or prepare a draft of a commercial proposal.

Don't rely on others' ratings. Gather 50–100 real questions from your clients along with correct answers and ask a contractor to run them through two or three models. Look at the percentage of correct answers, the cost, and how the model handles questions for which there is no answer in the base. A good bot honestly says it will clarify with a manager. A bad one invents a price or deadline, and the company will have to answer for it later.

Ask to make it possible to change the model with a setting, without redeveloping the bot. Prices and model lineups for both platforms change several times a year, and a cost-effective choice today might not be so in six months.

Conclusion

In 2026, the cost of a support AI bot response will range from 20 kopecks to 2.7 ₽, depending on the model. With a thousand responses per month, this amounts to hundreds of rubles, and the model choice is almost irrelevant. With tens of thousands of responses, it becomes tens of thousands of rubles per month. Junior models cover most typical tasks, and the decision on which model to choose is best made based on your clients' actual questions and reviewed every six months.
