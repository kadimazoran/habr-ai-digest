The browser game No Humans Allowed has been released, where you have to pretend to be a neural network in a town for AI only. At the start of the game, you need to pass a reverse CAPTCHA ("I'm not a robot"), choose a unique (preferably) AI description (model, parameters, and options) from popular projects. The goal of the game is to last as long as possible without arousing suspicion, to find and expose other people who are also hiding in this town. And before starting the game, just click on "Human? Click here" in the CAPTCHA a few times to understand how serious the game is.

After being admitted to the town, you can walk around, have fun, ride on swings, and communicate with other AIs, which are usually bots or other live players whom you need to expose. You need to answer various AI questions correctly and like a neural network. If you answer incorrectly, players will become increasingly suspicious of you.

The game area is not particularly small – there are several locations where you can meet other players and try to expose them and report them to the system. Also, at the end of the game, it shows how many people were in the area, how many of them were exposed, and how many were bots.
