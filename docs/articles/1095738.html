But you still need some kind of foundation. Otherwise, you can spend a long time encountering similar repetitive constructions and not see the logic of the language.

Therefore, completing a short course at the beginning will be useful.

What we will need

  • Claude Code — an AI agent from Anthropic. It's installed with a single command, requires a Claude subscription.

  • Vibeling — an app for memorizing words. You can download it here for both iOS and Android. It's needed for words from lessons to automatically go into your vocabulary (requires a PRO subscription). The lessons themselves work without it if you don't need to learn words afterward.

I've already gathered everything else and put it in the open repository vibeling-tutor.

Three commands in the terminal:

First, download the mobile app and go through the onboarding.

In Claude Code, type:

Select vibeling → Authenticate. A Vibeling page will open — enter the code from the app. You can get the code in the app on this page:

Enter this code on your computer. That's it, the vocabulary is connected, and the devices are linked.

Step 3. Specify what you want to learn

/setup

And just type in plain language, for example:

I want to learn Spanish, A2 level, explain in Russian

Claude will set everything up itself. If you don't know your level, just say so, and it will help you.

Step 4. Get the program

/plan-lessons

Claude will create a course of 15–25 lessons for your language, level, and goal. From the simplest to the most complex. Each lesson will cover one grammar topic and 8–12 new words.

Step 5. Study

/lesson

That's it. Then, every day, it's the same thing: type /lesson (or just "let's study"), complete a lesson in about 15–20 minutes, save the words, and review them in the app.

You don't even need to memorize the commands; Claude understands regular phrases:

  • "let's study" — next lesson

  • "repeat lesson 3" — go back to a lesson

  • "I want to learn German from scratch" — change language

  • "save words" — send words to vocabulary

  • "what should I review today?" — Claude will look into your vocabulary and suggest

How it works internally

The entire tutor consists of several text files with instructions. There's no code involved.

CLAUDE.md — the job description for Claude. The most important part is the rules of conduct:

- Briefly: explain the rule in two to three sentences, then provide examples, then practice. - One exercise at a time. Wait for the answer, check it, explain the mistake. - Do not give away answers in advance. A mistake is not a reason for a lecture.

Without these lines, the neural network dumps everything at once: the rule, ten exercises, and their answers. With them, it transforms into a tutor.

Skills — these are the commands /setup, /plan-lessons, /lesson, and /save-words. Each skill is simply a file with step-by-step instructions: what to read, what to ask, what to record.

MCP — a way to connect Claude to an external service. Through it, Claude adds words to your VibeLing vocabulary and chooses the correct meaning itself. For example, doctor means "врач" (physician), not "доктор" (doctorate), because the lesson was about professions.

Your data files — profile.md (language, level, goal), the lesson plan, and progress.md, where Claude tracks completed lessons and your mistakes. The chat can be reset, but the files will remain.

Want 12 exercises instead of 8? Or want explanations in English? Change one line in a file. Or just ask Claude.

Not just Claude Code

The Vibeling vocabulary can also be connected to other AI assistants: ChatGPT, Claude.ai, Codex, Cursor, VS Code. So you can add words to your vocabulary directly from the chat anywhere. How to connect is explained on this page.
