Before AI, I used to quit many habits that required regular logging after 10-20 days. Whether it was calorie counting, personal finance tracking, or regular task tracking.

For example, I've been trying to track my finances since I was 16. I've tried numerous apps, habit trackers, and various spreadsheets.

And while I could stick with it for the first couple of weeks, I'd eventually give up.

The scenario was always the same

For the first 7 days, you track everything enthusiastically.

Then you start skipping 1-2 days and keep the unrecorded data in your head. And some data is already forgotten.

And with each day, this mass of unrecorded information grew, and in the end, I never returned to my spreadsheets/apps again.

It was the same with food and calorie counting. I've returned to it many times, but it's even worse than tracking finances.

You have to photograph labels and assess what goes into the next dish. Plus, I constantly live in different countries where familiar products change, and labels are written in languages I don't understand.

And here's the huge advantage of AI – it reduces Friction in many tasks to almost zero.

Before, to record expenses, I had to:

  • find a moment when it's convenient for me to sit down at the computer/phone,

  • recall all receipts and expenses/income

  • and enter them manually

What if you have multiple cards? What if you paid partly with cash? What if you split a large bill with friends? These are all cognitive tasks that, over time, you just give up on and stop tracking anything.

Now, I just dictate a voice message / send photos to the agent. And it does everything else itself.

Essentially, this is the same advice from Atomic Habits:

Make the habit easy and remove all unnecessary steps.

Only, for me to finally follow it, I had to wait a few years until personal agents appeared in the world that would reduce the complexity of data logging to almost zero.

For example, I've been tracking my finances for 580 days

The first entry in my financial database appeared on April 14, 2025. Since then, I haven't missed a single day.

Now I can get an overview of all my income/expenses at any time and in any country.

It's the same with calories for me

I've been tracking them continuously for 33 days.

Not as long as with finances, but I only recently started.
Before, I wouldn't have lasted more than a week.

And another non-obvious idea

The agent records raw data much better than I do.

If I were recording manually, I would record the bare minimum: "lunch, 1000 calories." But the agent doesn't mind logging in RAW format. If I send it a voice message, a label, or a receipt, it will record everything:

  • what it is, where, when

  • how much it cost

  • a full breakdown of calories and macronutrients

And if something is missing, it will search for it on the internet itself.

Look how superficially I dictate to it, and it tries.
It even found the drink and added it correctly ⤵️

As a result, you get not just a daily figure, but a detailed breakdown of each day. And it's much easier to work with such data retrospectively.

Essentially, with a personal agent, everyone starts collecting their own dataset.

And this applies to a huge number of routine tasks that I couldn't do for more than a month before. Simply because each time I had to remember what needed to be recorded. And then find time to record it.

For example, my Stargaze or collecting analytics for all my platforms.

And in addition

With the advent of my personal agent, I have less "must not forget" in my head.

  • reply to someone

  • make a payment

  • return to an idea that came to mind on the road.

  • set myself a task and a reminder

Before, each such thought had to be transferred to the right application; now, I immediately give it to the agent. And it will put everything where it needs to go.

It turns out that now I don't need discipline where routine used to demand it.

So, that's how it is 💌
