How Ahrefs Counts Brand Mentions in ChatGPT

Ahrefs counts one response if the brand is mentioned at least once. In the Ahrefs study on ChatGPT from November 11, 2026, the authors used 5,516,381 responses from a US-based response database collected by Brand Radar. The queries cover all topics; Ahrefs describes them as real, based on search data.

If ChatGPT mentions Walmart three times in one response, the brand gets one credit. Walmart and walmart.com are also merged. The appearance of a domain in the text does not yet mean a link to it as a source.

To assess promotion, it is worth distinguishing between:

• Mention: ChatGPT named the brand, regardless of context.

• Recommendation: ChatGPT suggested choosing a company or its product for the user's task.

• Source link: ChatGPT indicated the page on which the answer is based.

The brand ranking in ChatGPT reflects the first indicator. The leader Walmart is mentioned in 95,605 responses, almost twice as often as Target in second place. But even Walmart appears in less than 2% of the entire database: it includes recipes, health questions, and other topics. Comparing this share to a store's result on questions about buying electronics is meaningless: the percentages are calculated on different sets of queries.

Google's seventh place is particularly telling: according to Ahrefs' analysis, the company is hardly involved in purchase-related responses. It would be too generous to attribute this to attracting new customers.

With Best Buy, the distinction is subtler. ChatGPT might recommend a specific iPad and mention the price at Best Buy nearby. The manufacturer gets a product recommendation, the seller gets a mention in a purchase-related response. The store's name next to the price is not equivalent to a recommendation to choose it specifically.

I consider roles more useful than a general ranking position: for a marketer, it's important at what stage the company is mentioned. When choosing a company from the top, advice to contact them is needed; when setting up a device, help for the owner is needed.

Why a Brand Mention in ChatGPT Doesn't Always Indicate a Product Choice

Responses to queries mentioning a brand should be considered separately from responses where the user is still choosing a manufacturer. Suppose someone asks if a specific iPhone is worth buying: the product is already specified in the query. In a request to choose a phone without specifying a manufacturer, the appearance of Apple has a different meaning. For promotion assessment, I would divide these groups: in one, the product is already known; in the other, it appears among options.

According to Ahrefs' calculations, stores and retail chains occupy 23 places in the top 100 and receive 35% of mentions from its participants. Automakers get 14%, mainly in questions about reliability, resale value, and car selection.

Brand awareness and the composition of queries must be considered before linking a ranking position to promotion. To test a specific tactic, responses to your audience's queries are needed.

How to Test Promotion in ChatGPT with Russian-Language Queries

For a Russian website, it is worth separately checking product selection, seller search, and post-purchase support for your audience's queries.

1. Formulate questions for each task. Training examples: which robot vacuum cleaner to choose for a pet-friendly apartment; where to buy the chosen robot vacuum cleaner with delivery; why the device does not connect to the network. Each role corresponds to a specific group of Russian-language queries.

2. Separate queries with and without the brand name. In the first group, record what ChatGPT says about the already named company; in the second, whether it appears among the options.

3. Repeat the same queries and save the full responses. As a starting point, you can use the recommendation from Visiobrand: 20 queries with three repetitions each. For comparison, record the date, available information about the ChatGPT version, and the search mode.

4. Mark mentions, recommendations, and links with separate fields. Save the wording around the brand name: a purchase recommendation and a price from a seller require different evaluations. Calculate shares from the number of responses in your specific query group.

5. Correlate responses with pages. Test the hypothesis about instructions by asking about product usage: is the brand mentioned, are its instructions linked. For a store, check queries about the place of purchase: is it mentioned and what conditions are provided. For a manufacturer, check if the product itself is recommended and why it is chosen.

After making changes to the website, compare results on the saved set of queries, considering the variance between repetitions. An increase in mentions by itself does not prove the effect of a specific edit: both the brand's role and the sources of the answer are important.

If the audience uses ChatGPT, GigaChat, Alice AI, and Perplexity, a separate result is needed for each service on the same Russian-language queries.

The common features of the ranking leaders are useful as a basis for checks, but they do not prove the effectiveness of specific promotion tactics. For a Russian website, a marketer makes decisions based on responses to Russian-language queries from their audience: does the brand get the desired role when choosing a product, a seller, or for post-purchase support?
