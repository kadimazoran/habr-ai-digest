There are no perfect tools for all tasks

Alas, the main naive illusion is the hope of finding one magic button for all occasions. It doesn't exist.

The speech technology market is a tough compromise. Convenient cloud solutions with beautiful interfaces can burn hundreds of dollars on per-minute rates. Platforms with fixed per-file payment save budget but can struggle with complex speaker interruptions. Local utilities guarantee privacy but require decent hardware. And cheap APIs require coding skills (and maintenance).

We are not singling anyone out and do not intend to sell you someone else's subscriptions. Look at your file formats, test the free limits on your own audio, and choose what solves your task.
