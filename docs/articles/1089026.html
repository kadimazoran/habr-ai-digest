How to negotiate within a team, navigate conflicts, and use AI in project work? Moderators of INFOSTART A&PM EVENT 2026 Ekaterina Zimnukhova, Semen Sinelshchikov, and Alexander Pishchalnikov discussed how these issues are connected in the conference program.

Communication Affects Deadlines and Results

Ekaterina Zimnukhova links the quality of interaction to the team's state. The inability to resolve conflicts and resist manipulation leads to burnout, and then:

"Deadlines will be missed and problems will arise."

Therefore, the "Soft Skills, Project Team Management" section is dedicated to situations affecting daily work: delegation, conflicts, manipulation, and decision-making.

The More AI, The More Important Live Communication

Semen Sinelshchikov noted the moderators' unexpected interest in communication. In his opinion, the spread of agents that act on behalf of specialists increases the need for relationships within the team:

"People want to spend more time on live communication."

Practical sessions will cover facilitation and making difficult decisions in meetings. In his own report on effective 1-on-1s, Sinelshchikov will analyze which questions help move beyond superficial conversation and conclude the meeting with concrete agreements.

AI Should Help the Team

Alexander Pishchalnikov said that AI submissions were balanced by cases of successful and unsuccessful implementations. His approach is to use technologies to:

"Provide our employees and colleagues with convenient tools."

Alexander will base his own report on an audit of an automation project: customer and integrator mistakes, time and money losses, and the role of independent expertise. Among other topics of interest to him are resolving conflicts with customers and within the team for the sake of project results.

How the Program is Structured

INFOSTART A&PM EVENT 2026 will take place from November 12–14 in St. Petersburg, at the Cosmos Saint-Petersburg Pribaltiyskaya hotel.

Practical formats make up 70% of the program: master classes, training sessions, round tables, management duels, and business games. Another 30% consists of reports with case studies, error analysis, and tools.

The conference is designed for 1C analysts, project managers, and product teams. Five sections cover the tools and applied competencies of an analyst, 1C solutions, project and product management, communication, and teamwork. You can choose topics and formats that suit your needs on the conference website.
