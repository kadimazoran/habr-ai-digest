Today we'll try to generate some low-poly 'characters' and convert them into 3D using AI. We'll compare generations from Nano Banana Pro, Krea 2, and Microsoft MAI-Image-2.5, and see how some of them translate into 3D.

I took medieval fantasy with its painfully overused tropes best genre canons as inspiration. There are five characters: a goblin merchant, a dark knight, a crossbow cat, a minotaur, and a wandering sorceress. Since 3D generators offer limited free attempts and you can't download them without a subscription, I'll have to pick and choose which images to convert to 3D.

The prompt was universal and included the instruction that the character should be low-poly. Nano Banana did the best job, in my opinion, although the goblin from Krea 2 looks quite interesting—such a model would fit some psychedelic, parody dark quest, but not a conventionally 'standard' game in the chosen setting. The Microsoft goblin turned out a bit too polished.
