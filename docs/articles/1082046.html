OpenAI recently released GPT-6 Astra. The new model has improved its ability to handle long and complex tasks, large contexts, files, and tools. For me, this is an interesting release also because I primarily use AI not for writing code. I'm a PM, so my main tasks involve requirements, Jira, documents, information analysis, planning, and preparing materials.

Over the past year, my approach to AI has changed quite a bit. At first, ChatGPT was an improved search engine for me: give me information, and I'll process it myself. Now, I increasingly give it a whole chain of actions: gather information, structure it, find patterns, verify them, and draw conclusions. If a large amount of independent work with files is needed afterward, I can transfer the already formed task to Codex.

With the advent of Astra, another question has arisen: which model should I even give a specific task to? The most powerful model is not always necessary.

I'll preface this by saying: this is not an instruction manual for working with AI, nor an attempt to compile an encyclopedia of ChatGPT, Codex, and OpenAI models. I simply want to share how I personally use these tools in my PM work, what has stuck, and what hasn't. If even a couple of ideas from this are useful to someone and become part of their daily work, then the article was written for a reason. I'll be glad 🙂

First, I go to Chat. And not always to Codex afterward

Suppose I need to understand a set of requirements. There's Jira, related tasks, old comments. Some things are already implemented, and the current solution might be in a completely different task.

Now, I start with Chat more often. And often, I don't even type, but just speak out loud what's happening and what I want to figure out. When the task isn't yet formulated, it's much more convenient this way. Then I ask it to gather information, find contradictions, and I check the conclusions: where did this requirement come from, is there a source, is it still relevant?

When I understand the picture myself, I ask Chat to prepare instructions for Codex. It turns into a funny chain: I explain to Chat with my voice what I roughly want, and Chat then explains to Codex what exactly needs to be done 🙂
