Overall, both models handled the 3D-ification of the drawings more or less equally, although Meshy, in my opinion, produces a rougher result. Its blocking barriers are also disappointing, especially when the content itself does not contain anything criminal.

What is pleasing, however, is the noticeable consistency in the drawings from Krea 2. This model generates slower than the others and for some reason doesn't always grasp the reference immediately, drawing not quite what's needed. But the stylistic consistency in successive generations is quite good.
