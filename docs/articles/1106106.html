How to formulate requirements, find contradictions before development begins, and implement AI agents into workflows? These and other questions were discussed at the "Analyst's Code" meetup by Yandex Money.

Speakers from Yandex Money, Sber, and "Byndyusoft" presented at the meeting. They shared their practical experience in system analysis: from working with requirements and stakeholders to automating tasks with AI. What was discussed at the meetup

  • Practices for writing technical solutions: creating a glossary and a unified requirements space, identifying conflicts before development, storing the context of decisions made.

  • To bring an AI agent from prototype to production, an analyst turns an idea into a verifiable specification: what work the agent should do, what actions are allowed, how the result is confirmed, and when it should be handed over to a human.

  • If the architecture is described by code, its problems are code problems. And tests are written for code problems.

  • An AI agent assists the analyst throughout the entire process from requirements to development support. For this assistance to become a stable practice, clear and complete rules of operation are needed, points for manual verification of conclusions by a human, and the transfer of successful scenarios to the team for general use.
