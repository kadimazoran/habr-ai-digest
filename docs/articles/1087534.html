1. Edge Document Recognition

Imagine: a shipment arrives at the warehouse, the courier brings a folder of documents — invoices, acts, bills, TORG-12, accompanying forms, and others. What to do?

😶 Previously, the document had to be sent to where it could be processed: by an operator, in the back office, or the general department. There, information was manually transferred into the information system. The more documents, the more people, time, and infrastructure this process required.

We at Smart Engines took a different path. We created edge recognition technology: for entering document data on-site, without sending it to a data processing center.

2. How Edge Recognition Works and What It Gives to Business

How does edge recognition work?

Edge recognition turns a document into structured data directly at the moment and point of its appearance, without sending it to a processing center and waiting in line. Moreover, the entire process — from document classification to extracting structured data and verifying signatures and seals — is performed directly on the end device.

Smart Document Engine capabilities for edge recognition:

  • Automatic document classification

  • Recognition of printed and handwritten text

  • Verification of signatures and seals

  • Operation in real-world conditions

  • Explainable results

What effect does this have on business?

✅ Edge recognition from Smart Engines and B1's business expertise help companies not just speed up document processing but reduce it to a single step. Data is extracted directly at the moment of operation — on any device and in any location.

Control of goods, reconciliation of nomenclature, verification of signatures, and creation of a digitized document with a text layer for integration into the archive are performed on-site, in seconds, and without manual entry.

And general departments and data processing centers can focus on handling exceptions and inter-departmental communication.

5. Tables, Frames, and the Credit Pipeline

Errors in invoice tables are too costly for businesses

And finding and correcting them is one of the most labor-intensive tasks in management accounting. With a flow of 1000 invoices per month, the costs due to incorrect data can exceed 1.7 million rubles 💵

Up to 30-40% of all discrepancies when reconciling documents with orders are in the tabular part, and errors are found in approximately 39% of invoices. An incorrectly specified name, quantity, VAT rate, or price — and instead of automatic processing, employees are forced to look for discrepancies and make corrections manually again.

In the new version of Smart Document Engine 3.5, the quality of working with tables has reached a level where manual data entry is no longer needed 🔥

A quarter reduction in errors has made fully automatic processing of invoices, UРD, waybills, TORG-12, and other documents with tabular structures possible.

Just photograph the document or upload a scan: the platform automatically finds table boundaries, extracts data, and reconstructs their structure — even with angled shots, poor lighting, and distortions.

As a result, data entry, reconciliation, and transfer to accounting systems are performed automatically in real-time — without line-by-line manual verification and retroactive corrections.

For data processing centers, this means a transition from the role of a manual data entry center to an expertise and control center 🔥
