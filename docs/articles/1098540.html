A character sheet is needed to better understand who our hero or heroine is. It serves as a reference for the game development team and helps keep character improvement more or less in "unison."

Today, we will try to create high-quality character sheets for fantasy game characters using three models: Grok Image 2.0, Krea 2, and Nano Banana Pro.

#1. Wandering Sorceress

For the starting point of this character, a sorceress from Warcraft III was taken. However, for some reason, all three models did not fail to add a pointed Gandalf-like hat to her wardrobe – apparently, AI models also obey the collective unconscious.

Regarding the character's detail and characteristics, Grok undoubtedly leads, as it not only shows the sorceress from different angles but even goes through the description of her inventory and provides a summary of the color palette. Although Krea, ahem, has certainly outdone herself – apparently, her censorship filters are weaker than those of other commercial models.

Looking at all three models, one can involuntarily hear the insidious whisper, "I will give my life for the King of the Dead..." Krea, again for some reason, went into rampant eroticization, and her low-poly 3D style, in this case, looks the most consistent and convincing.
