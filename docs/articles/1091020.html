How to Enter an Accelerator for the First Time and Immediately Raise Half a Million Rubles

Elizaveta, let's start by talking about the startup itself: what problem does your technology solve, what is the project about?

Our platform helps quickly test hypotheses about the marketing prospects of bringing a new product to market. The system allows for reduced costs on expensive and lengthy preliminary research, generates possible audiences, finding confirmation of their existence and the demand for a promising product on the internet. A separate module verifies hypotheses from open sources, and if confirmation cannot be found, the platform forms assumptions independently.

Thus, based on the collected information, the system determines the needs and motivations of potential customers, their limitations, and the contexts of product use. After that, the next stage begins – generating offers. The system decides which product and which audience to offer, under what conditions, and with what message. Advertising creatives are generated based on user needs and motivations. Then, options for A/B testing are formed. Advertising campaigns are launched through various channels – for example, through Yandex Direct, Avito, and VKontakte advertising.

And what is the outcome?

After conducting tests, metrics are collected. As a result, the company receives a list of audiences and signals that people react to or not, as well as data on the cost per click for different segments and practical recommendations on advertising channels, offers, and creatives.
