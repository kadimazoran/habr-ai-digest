Qwen4-Max-Preview is a new flagship model from Alibaba. It was released on November 11, 2026, as a preview. Access to the model can be obtained through QwenCloud, Qwen APP, Qoder, QwenWork, QwenStudio, and other Qwen platforms. Token-based payments have not been implemented yet.

The Personal Lite subscription costs $6 per month, and for new users, there is a limited-time offer for $3 (until November 30, and only for those who have not previously purchased a personal plan). The plan includes 20,000 credits per month, and their consumption depends on the model, token volume, reasoning mode, and tool calls, so a $3–$6 unlimited plan should not be expected.

During the preview period, credit consumption for this model is several times lower than usual (up to 80% discount), and at night Beijing time, from 10 PM to 8 AM, the discount reaches 92%. The preview ends on November 25, and the model will be disabled. Alibaba has not announced the date for the full release.

Words are there. But benchmarks are not

The announcement of Qwen4's release on X (formerly Twitter) contains no specific numbers, only generalized facts about the new model – faster, higher, stronger, more efficient.

In the found documentation, Qwen4-Max is called a next-generation flagship multimodal model. It calls functions, searches the internet, uses built-in tools, and can respond strictly in JSON. The model is compatible with OpenAI and Anthropic Messages APIs, so it can be connected to Claude Code, Cursor, and other programming agents via a separate address and key.

Conclusions

Currently, Qwen3.8-Max, released to the public on August 3, remains a proven stable model, while Qwen4-Max-Preview is still a newcomer without user experience. To decide on a favorite, they need to be tested on real tasks, and it's best to do so before November 25, before the preview closes.

Alibaba already showcased the Qwen4 lineup – Max, Plus, Flash, and 27B – at the Apsara conference on September 22, but did not provide timelines or specifications. It has not been officially confirmed whether the 27 billion parameter version will be open for local deployment. What the other models in the lineup can do will become clear after their release.
