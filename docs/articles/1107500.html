Anthropic paid OnePWR Solutions $3.4 billion. Previously, such sums were seen in chip supplies, but this time it's about gas generators to produce 1.7 GW of power for data centers. According to the Financial Times, this is the largest single purchase of energy equipment by an AI company.

We are already accustomed to the shortage of AI chips, especially high-performance memory and GPUs, with supplies scheduled until 2030. Now it's the turn of the resources to run these chips. The current energy capacities in the US have proven very limited, and the launch of AI centers is causing protests from local residents, for whom the cost of energy is increasing manifold – it's more profitable for suppliers to sell it to data center owners. Connection times to power grids are stretching out to the same 2030. To shorten them, AI companies are switching to autonomous generation and are even purchasing used aircraft turbines.

It is noteworthy that Anthropic is choosing not "green" energy sources, but gas and nuclear energy. Data centers require constant energy; it is unacceptable to depend on cloudiness and wind strength. And possible carbon emission penalties will simply become another line item in expenses.

Other AI companies have the same approach: Elon Musk's SpaceX uses gas turbines for its Colossus series supercomputers in Tennessee and Mississippi, and Meta* is developing its own gas generation in Ohio. Microsoft, Amazon, and Google are restarting US nuclear power to obtain a constant energy source. For example, Google has invested over $4.4 billion in Constellation Energy to modernize existing nuclear power plants and obtain an additional 890 MW of capacity over the next 20 years.

Nikita Tsaplin, CEO of the cloud provider RUVDS, explained the trend in a WEALTH Navigator club column: computation is ceasing to be a purely digital story and is hitting physical limits – generation, networks, cooling, and equipment placement. When an AI company buys power plants, it is effectively vertically integrating into the energy sector: GPUs without megawatts are expensive metal, not a product. As a result, in the AI race, energy is becoming a strategic asset just like chips.

*Meta's activities are banned in Russia as extremist.
