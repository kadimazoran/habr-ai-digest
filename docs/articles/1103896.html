Hello, Habr residents!

In less than a decade, deep learning has gone through two key stages. First, Python libraries like Keras, TensorFlow, and PyTorch brought neural networks from laboratory experiments into real production. Then, LLMs and other generative AI tools transformed business and society. François Chollet, creator of the Keras library, and Matthew Watson, who works on Gemini at Google, have completely reworked and updated the book so you can understand how transformers work, build your own mini-GPT model and a diffusion image generation model, and fine-tune a language model using the LoRA method.

What's inside:

  • code examples for each topic covered;

  • broad coverage — from fundamentals to generative AI;

  • accessible explanations of the mathematics behind the methods;

  • code in Keras, TensorFlow, PyTorch, and JAX.

The book is suitable for those who have intermediate Python skills and want to understand deep learning — from the basics to modern generative models, without being tied to a single tool and without special mathematical training.

About the Authors
François Chollet

has been working in deep learning since 2012, when it began to gain popularity in academia. He is the creator of Keras, one of the most popular deep learning libraries, used in universities, major companies like Google, Netflix, and Spotify, and renowned scientific organizations like CERN and NASA. François is one of the founders of the Ndea research lab, which develops advanced artificial intelligence systems, and the creator of the ARC-AGI benchmark for measuring machine intelligence levels.

Matthew Watson

has been working in machine learning at Google since 2018, where he participated in the development of the Gemini model and Google's open deep learning ecosystem. He is one of the main developers of Keras, specializing in natural language processing tools. He holds a Master's degree in Computer Science from Stanford University, where he researched procedural modeling techniques at the Stanford Graphics Lab.
