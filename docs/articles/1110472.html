SKILLOUT returns with its II season – and an online league for teams from anywhere in the world. On November 28–29 in Moscow, teams with neural networks will create advertising videos for brands live within 24 hours.

GPTunneL provides teams with access to AI models. For participants of the online league, which is launching for the first time, we are offering free access to Grom.

We'll tell you about the format and dates.

What is SKILLOUT

It is a series of hackathons on AI content.

Advertising format: a brand provides a brief (task, product, audience, tone), and the team creates one to three videos up to a minute long in 24 hours. Winners are chosen by a jury and brand representatives. This season, briefs are provided by OTP Bank, Nutricia, and GPTunneL.

In the first season (March 2026), there were 24 teams, 72 participants, and 35 videos created in 24 hours; 64 experts discussed it live.

What teams will have at their disposal

  • The GPTunneL platform offers over 100 AI tools: ChatGPT, Claude, Midjourney, Kling, Suno, Mureka, MiniMax, and others. For the offline league, these are available without limits.
