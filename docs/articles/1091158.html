The 3SUM Problem

Does a given set of numbers contain three that sum to zero?

This is the entire problem. It's formulated in a single sentence, and generally, any reasonably trained programmer can solve it using a hash table and nested loops in five minutes:

Claude's Role

The authors of the preprint report that during the search for solutions to open cryptographic problems, an Anthropic employee, after generating 16 million tokens, found the algorithm described in the preprint. In September 2026, Anthropic shared it with the authors, along with an NDA, money, and free access to public models.

After writing the paper, Anthropic also used its internal model to verify the results using the Lean 4 theorem proving system — the formalization is also available online.

The authors also indicate that they used Claude's assistance in writing the text, creating illustrations (there are 10 of them in the paper), and verifying mathematical facts.
