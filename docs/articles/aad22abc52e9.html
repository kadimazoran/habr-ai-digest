Various AI assistants have been around in fintech for a long time; every banking app has at least one. But what will they become when they "grow up"? We at SVOY Tech decided to explore the future of AI in financial services.

Through a survey, we want to understand in what form AI in fintech will be in demand by users in the next five years, and then look at the same ideas through the eyes of developers: how difficult and expensive it is to implement AI, what prevents a solution from moving from pilot to production, and what architectures look realistic.

In the first part of the survey, we invite you to reflect on the future of fintech from the perspective of a bank or fintech service user. In the second part, as a specialist evaluating the development and implementation of an AI-based product.

We will collect your answers and analyze them. The results will be published later in our blog.
